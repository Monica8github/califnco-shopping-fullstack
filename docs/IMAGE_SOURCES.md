# Product imagery and demonstration catalogue

Prepared 23 October 2026. This repository contains 60 **mock catalogue SKUs**, ten in each of six categories. Prices, stock, dealer discounts, bundle quantities and technical descriptions are demonstration data; they are not manufacturer offers or verified performance specifications.

The 60 SKUs use 18 representative product-family photographs. Pack variants intentionally reuse the corresponding single-unit photograph. Their names, descriptions and Pack quantity field make this explicit. These are 60 catalogue entries, not 60 independently photographed designs.

Public manufacturer/supplier photos are used as reference imagery for this technical assignment. They are not generated images, do not establish a Califnco manufacturer relationship, and carry no representation of permission for commercial reuse. Before launching a real store, replace them with merchant-owned/licensed assets and verified actual inventory. Manufacturer marks visible inside source photos belong to their owners.

Product naming is generic and mock; images are matched by physical component category. Compatibility, capacities, certifications, health benefits and warranties are deliberately not asserted.

## Asset handling

Files were downloaded from the image URLs exposed by the linked official pages/public product feeds. Shopify assets were requested at an 800-pixel server-delivered width where available. No logos were removed, and no new product details were painted into the photos. The local files keep the original photo format.

For mobile clients, resolve `/products/...` against the deployed admin/API origin; for offline/mobile bundling use the same files as bundled Expo assets.
